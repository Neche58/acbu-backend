## 1. Introduction

ACBU (African Currency Basket Unit) is a multi-currency stablecoin pegged to a basket of
African fiat currencies and backed 1:1 by on-chain reserves. It is designed to reduce
cross-border friction, provide a stable store of value, and enable programmable finance
across African markets.

---

## 2. Token Economics

ACBU is minted when users deposit fiat currency and burned when they redeem it. Total
supply is always fully collateralised by the reserve basket. No algorithmic stabilisation
is used.

---

## 3. Reserve Model

Reserves are held in segregated custody accounts per currency. The reserve ratio must
remain at or above 1.02 (102 %) at all times. The reserve tracker contract publishes
on-chain proof of reserves every 24 hours.

---

## 4. Currency Basket

The initial basket comprises Nigerian Naira (NGN), Kenyan Shilling (KES), South African
Rand (ZAR), Ghanaian Cedi (GHS), and Egyptian Pound (EGP). Basket weights are rebalanced
weekly by the oracle committee.

---

## 5. Minting

Users deposit fiat via supported fintech rails (Flutterwave, Paystack, MTN MoMo). Once
the deposit is confirmed, ACBU tokens are minted on Stellar Soroban and credited to the
user's wallet.

---

## 6. Burning

Users initiate a burn to redeem ACBU for fiat. Tokens are burned on-chain before the
fiat payout is issued.

---

## 7. Transfers

Peer-to-peer ACBU transfers settle on Stellar within 3–5 seconds. International
transfers to non-ACBU wallets are routed via Stellar Anchor integrations.

---

## 8. Savings & Yield

Users may lock ACBU into the Savings Vault to earn yield. Interest accrues daily and is
credited monthly.

---

## 9. Lending Pool

Verified users (Tier 2+) may borrow against ACBU collateral via the Lending Pool
contract. Collateral ratio must remain above 150 %.

---

## 10. P2P Payments

ACBU supports QR-code and phone-number-based P2P payments within the same jurisdiction
and cross-border.

---

## 11. Enterprise & SME

The Enterprise segment provides bulk payroll, supplier payments, and API-based
disbursement with dedicated rate limits and KYC for corporate accounts.

### §12.2 Fee Table (All Fee Types)

|----------|--------|-------|
| Mint fee | 0.30 % – 1.00 % | Dynamic, based on reserve ratio (see §12.1) |

#### §12.2a Burn Fee Tiers

| Reserve Weight (% of target) | Fee (BPS) | Fee (%) |
|------------------------------|-----------|---------|
| < 85 % | 200 | 2.00 % |

| > 115 % | 5 | 0.05 % |

### §12.3 Float Interest (Savings Vault)

The Savings Vault pays a target APY in the range **8 % – 12 %**, derived from the
yield generated by deploying idle reserves into African money-market instruments and
short-term government securities.

Revenue projection at $50 M AUM (illustrative):

| AUM | APY Assumption | Gross Annual Interest Income |
|-----|---------------|------------------------------|
| $50 M | 10 % | ~$5.0 M |
| $20 M | 10 % | ~$2.0 M |
| $10 M | 10 % | ~$1.0 M |

> The 8 %–12 % range reflects observed yield variability across target markets.
> The protocol retains a 0–2 % management spread; the remainder is paid to depositors.
> All projections are illustrative and not a guarantee of return.

## 14. Governance

### §14.1 Phased Governance Model

ACBU governance evolves in three phases to balance immediate operational needs with
long-term decentralisation.

| Phase | Period | Governing Body | Decision Mechanism |
|-------|--------|----------------|--------------------|
| Phase 1 – Founding Board | Launch → Year 2 | 7-member Founding Board | Simple majority (4 of 7) |
| Phase 2 – Hybrid | Year 2 → Year 3 | Founding Board + elected community delegates | On-chain advisory votes; binding ratification by Board |
| Phase 3 – DAO | Year 3+ | Token-weighted DAO | On-chain governance contract; Founding Board holds veto only for security emergencies |

### §14.2 Founding Board Composition

The Founding Board comprises 7 individuals elected at the genesis ceremony. Board seats
rotate on 2-year terms. A supermajority (5 of 7) is required for:

- Changing basket composition
- Adjusting reserve ratio thresholds
- Protocol upgrades affecting tokenomics

### §14.3 Community Participation (Phase 2+)

In Phase 2, community delegates are elected proportionally by staked ACBU holders.
Advisory votes are non-binding but publicly recorded on-chain. The Founding Board must
provide a written rationale when overriding an advisory vote.

### §14.4 DAO (Phase 3 — Year 3+)

From Year 3, protocol governance moves to a token-weighted on-chain DAO. Proposals
require a minimum 1 % quorum of circulating supply and a 7-day voting period. The
Founding Board retains a time-locked emergency veto (48-hour window) to block
proposals that endanger user funds.

> **Note:** DAO governance is **not yet in effect**. Current decisions are made by the
> Founding Board under Phase 1. The DAO transition timeline is subject to regulatory
> approval and community readiness review.

## 15. KYC & Compliance

### §15.1 KYC Overview

All users must complete KYC before transacting. ACBU uses a tiered verification model
administered by a decentralised network of accredited validators.

### §15.2 KYC Tiers and Transaction Limits

| Tier | Name | Required Documents | Daily Limit (USD eq.) | Monthly Limit (USD eq.) | Cumulative Balance |
|------|------|--------------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|--------------------|
| 0 | Unverified | None | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| 1 | Basic | Government ID + selfie | $500 | $2,000 | $5,000 |
| 2 | Standard | Tier 1 + proof of address | $5,000 | $20,000 | $50,000 |
| 3 | Enhanced | Tier 2 + source-of-funds declaration | $50,000 | $200,000 | Unlimited |

### §15.3 Travel Rule Compliance

ACBU applies FATF Travel Rule requirements based on the USD-equivalent transfer value:

| Threshold | Obligation |
|-----------|-----------|
| < $1,000 | No Travel Rule data required |
| ≥ $1,000 (FATF standard) | Originator and beneficiary data transmitted to receiving VASP |
| ≥ $3,000 (US FinCEN) | Full originator + beneficiary data; records retained ≥ 5 years |
| ≥ $10,000 | Automatic SAR consideration trigger |

Travel Rule data exchange uses the IVMS 101 data standard. ACBU integrates with
Notabene for inter-VASP communication.

### §15.4 KYC Fee

A non-refundable fee of **1 ACBU** is charged per application submission (all tiers).
The fee enters the validator reward pool and does not count toward protocol revenue.

### §15.5 Sanctions Screening

All KYC applications and transaction counterparties are screened against OFAC, EU, and
UN consolidated sanctions lists. Matches trigger an automatic hold and compliance review.

## 16. Security

See `SECURITY.md` for the vulnerability disclosure policy. Key security controls:
- HMAC-signed webhook payloads
- AES-256-GCM encryption for PII at rest
- JWT with short expiry + refresh-token rotation
- Brute-force protection on all authentication endpoints

## 19. Revenue Model

### §19.1 Revenue Streams

| Stream | Mechanism |
|--------|-----------|
| Mint / burn spread | Dynamic fee (§12.1, §12.2a) |
| FX spread | 0.25 % on buy/sell mid-rate |
| Float interest | Yield from reserve deployment (§19.2) |
| Enterprise SLA fees | Fixed monthly fee for API access tier |

### §19.2 Float Interest Revenue Projections

Idle reserves are deployed into African money-market instruments at a target yield of
**10 % APY** (flat assumption used for all projection scenarios below).

| AUM | APY | Annual Revenue |
|-----|-----|---------------|
| $10 M | 10 % | ~$1.0 M |
| $20 M | 10 % | ~$2.0 M |
| $50 M | 10 % | ~$5.0 M |

> The Savings Vault APY paid to depositors (§12.3) is drawn from this yield after
> deducting a 0–2 % protocol management spread. The 8 %–12 % depositor APY range
> reflects the variable net yield after this spread and market conditions.

## 24. Governance Detail

### §24.1 Proposal Process

Any holder of ≥ 10,000 ACBU (Phase 3+) may submit a governance proposal. Proposals
enter a 3-day discussion period before going to a vote.

### §24.2 Emergency Actions

The Founding Board (Phase 1 & 2) or the Emergency Council (Phase 3) may take
unilateral action within a 48-hour window to protect user funds. All emergency actions
are published on-chain immediately.

### §24.3 Fee and Parameter Changes

Fee parameters and tier limits are governance-adjustable from Phase 3 (Year 3+).
Until then, changes require Founding Board majority approval.

### §24.4 Current Governance Status

**ACBU is currently in Phase 1 (Founding Board governance).**

The platform is governed by a 7-member Founding Board under a simple-majority rule.
On-chain DAO governance (§14.4) is planned for Year 3 and is **not yet active**.
All fee changes, basket rebalancing decisions, and validator network parameters are
currently approved by the Founding Board.

Users and integrators should not assume DAO-level decentralisation until the Phase 3
transition is formally announced and ratified.
